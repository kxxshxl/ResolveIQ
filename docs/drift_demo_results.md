## What the alerts say

From the first trial of each scenario that detected the change (the table above gives how often that happens). The text is exactly what the API returns.

**intent_shift_25**: billing_dispute rises from ~16% to 25% of recent traffic. Not detected in any trial.

**intent_shift_33**: billing_dispute rises to 33%. Detected in trial 2 (seed 1001).
- intent mix shifted (p=6.8e-04, PSI 0.32): billing_dispute 14% to 33%, device_issue 18% to 9%, broadband_disconnection 10% to 3%

**intent_shift_40**: billing_dispute rises to 40%. Detected in trial 1 (seed 1000).
- intent mix shifted (p=3.6e-06, PSI 0.35): billing_dispute 15% to 40%, sim_mobile_connectivity 12% to 5%, plan_change 10% to 5%

**intent_shift_25_large**: billing_dispute rises to 25%, with 4x the traffic (1,200 baseline / 400 recent requests). Detected in trial 3 (seed 1002).
- intent mix shifted (p=9.0e-05, PSI 0.11): billing_dispute 15% to 25%, broadband_disconnection 10% to 5%, service_outage 9% to 5%

**severity_shift_40**: high/critical severity rises from ~30% to 40%. Not detected in any trial.

**severity_shift_55**: high/critical severity rises to 55%. Detected in trial 2 (seed 1001).
- severity mix shifted (p=2.8e-07, PSI 0.41): high 18% to 46%, medium 43% to 27%, low 32% to 18%

**new_topic_4**: 4 of 100 recent complaints (4%) are about a topic the corpus has never seen. Not detected in any trial.

**new_topic_6**: 6 of 100 (6%) are a new topic. Not detected in any trial.

**new_topic_10**: 10 of 100 (10%) are a new topic. Detected in trial 1 (seed 1000).
- 9 recent complaints form a new topic (app, streaming, says, channels): almost nothing like it appears in the baseline; no existing evidence explains it (p=0.001)

**new_topics_3x8**: three new topics at once, 8 complaints each (24% of recent traffic); detected = at least one recovered. Detected in trial 2 (seed 1001).
- evidence confidence fell (KS D=0.29, p=2.4e-04): mean 0.64 to 0.58, 10th percentile 0.54 to 0.48. Retrieval regression, stale knowledge base or vocabulary shift
- 8 recent complaints form a new topic (old, number, porting, transferred): almost nothing like it appears in the baseline; no existing evidence explains it (p=0.004)
- Discovery link: the proposal builder produced 1 proposal(s) from the same window. cluster c1 (8 complaints, old, number, porting) shares 5 complaints with a `new_class` proposal `old_number` (old, number, transferred, ago)

**vocabulary_shift**: every recent complaint arrives in chat-widget style (abbreviated, lower case). Detected in trial 1 (seed 1000).
- evidence confidence fell (KS D=0.35, p=2.4e-05): mean 0.63 to 0.57, 10th percentile 0.54 to 0.46. Retrieval regression, stale knowledge base or vocabulary shift
- abstention rose from 5% to 22% (p=3.5e-04): requests the corpus cannot answer are becoming common
- complaints moved in embedding space (centroid cosine distance 0.168, permutation p=0.002)

**stale_knowledge_base**: tickets for 3 of 9 intents vanish from the evidence corpus. Detected in trial 4 (seed 1003).
- evidence confidence fell (KS D=0.27, p=0.001): mean 0.63 to 0.60, 10th percentile 0.56 to 0.46. Retrieval regression, stale knowledge base or vocabulary shift
- abstention rose from 2% to 17% (p=6.5e-04): requests the corpus cannot answer are becoming common
